### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an Applied AI/ML Data Scientist with over 12 years of experience across financial regulation, central banking, and insurance, specializing in building scalable, governed Analytics & AI systems in regulated environments.

My work spans data and AI engineering, analytics engineering, and enterprise AI architecture, with a focus on translating complex business and regulatory requirements into secure, production-ready solutions.

My expertise includes generative AI, agentic AI systems, and context engineering, supported by experience in MLOps, LLMOps, and modern data engineering practices. I am particularly interested in embedding governance, security, and responsible AI principles directly into the design and deployment of enterprise AI systems.

My focus is on building and scaling intelligent, governed systems that strengthen prudential and financial risk management, improve decision-making, and deliver measurable business value.
